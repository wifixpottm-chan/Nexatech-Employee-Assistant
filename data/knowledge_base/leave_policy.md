# NexaTech GmbH Leave Policy

## Vacation
Full-time employees receive between 28 and 30 paid vacation days per calendar year depending on their employment contract. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated entitlement according to their contract.

## Vacation Requests
Vacation should normally be requested through the employee portal before the planned absence.

## Approval
Vacation is subject to manager approval and operational requirements.

## Sick Leave
Employees who are unable to work because of illness should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

## Leave Balance
Approved vacation days reduce the employee's remaining annual vacation balance. Pending requests do not reduce the balance until approved.
