# NexaTech GmbH Overtime Policy

## Standard Working Time
The standard full-time working week is 40 hours.

## Overtime
Hours worked beyond an employee's contractual weekly working hours may constitute overtime.

## Approval
Overtime should normally be approved by the employee's manager before it is performed.

## Recording
Employees must accurately record overtime through the company's time tracking system.

## Compensation
Approved overtime may be compensated through time off or another arrangement according to the employee's contract and company policy.
