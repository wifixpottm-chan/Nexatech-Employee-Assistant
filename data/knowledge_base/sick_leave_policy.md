# NexaTech GmbH Sick Leave Policy

## Notification
Employees who are unable to work due to illness should notify their manager as soon as possible.

## Attendance System
Employees should record their absence as sick leave according to the company's attendance procedure.

## Documentation
Medical documentation may be required depending on the duration of the absence and applicable company rules.

## Return to Work
Employees should inform their manager when they expect to return to work when required by the company's procedure.
